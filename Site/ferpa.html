FERPA Alignment

We align our practices with the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA) to support educational institutions in protecting student education records.

Our controls are designed to support lawful access, limited disclosure, and responsible handling of student information.

Student Privacy Protection

Access to education records is restricted to authorized personnel and governed by documented policies and contractual requirements.

Controlled Data Access

Role-based access controls help ensure that student data is accessed only for legitimate educational or administrative purposes.

Use & Disclosure Safeguards

Student information is used and disclosed only in accordance with institutional direction and applicable regulations.

Policy & Documentation

Written procedures support FERPA-aligned handling, retention, and oversight of education records.

Education Sector Support

Our alignment framework is designed to support K–12 districts, higher education institutions, and education service providers.

FERPA alignment is maintained through governance, training, and institutional coordination to support ongoing compliance obligations.
