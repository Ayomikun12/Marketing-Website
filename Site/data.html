Data Privacy

We are committed to responsible data handling practices that respect privacy, support regulatory obligations, and protect sensitive information entrusted to us.

Our approach emphasizes transparency, accountability, and alignment with public sector and educational expectations.

Our Privacy Approach

Data privacy is integrated into our operational, technical, and governance practices. We collect, process, and store information only as necessary to support contractual and service-related objectives.

Privacy considerations are addressed throughout the lifecycle of our services, from system design and access control to retention and disposal.

Data Handling Scope

We work with institutions to define data ownership, processing responsibilities, and oversight requirements through documented agreements and governance structures.

Personal and sensitive data is handled in accordance with applicable laws, contractual obligations, and internal policies.

Privacy Principles

Data access is restricted to authorized personnel based on role and responsibility.

Information is collected and processed only for defined and legitimate purposes.

Safeguards are implemented to protect against unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.

Data handling responsibilities are governed by contractual and policy-based controls.

Data privacy practices are designed to support regulatory and contractual obligations. Final compliance determinations remain the responsibility of the data-owning institution or agency.
