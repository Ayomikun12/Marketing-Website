Data Processing Addendum (DPA)

This Data Processing Addendum (“DPA”) governs the processing of personal data by authorized vendors, partners, and service providers supporting public sector and regulated industry operations.

The DPA establishes clear roles, responsibilities, and security requirements to ensure compliance with applicable data protection regulations and government data handling standards.

Purpose of This Addendum

This DPA defines how personal and sensitive data is processed, protected, stored, and transferred when delivering services to government agencies, educational institutions, and regulated public sector entities.

Scope of Processing

Vendors may only process personal data for purposes directly related to contracted service delivery and must follow documented government procurement and security policies.

Vendor Responsibilities

  • Process data only under documented customer instructions
  • Maintain appropriate technical and organizational safeguards
  • Ensure personnel confidentiality and security training
  • Support regulatory audits and compliance verification
  • Notify customers of security incidents without undue delay

Security Controls

Vendors must maintain industry-recognized security frameworks including encryption, access control, monitoring, and secure infrastructure management.

Sub-Processor Management

Vendors must disclose approved sub-processors and ensure equivalent contractual data protection obligations are enforced.

Data Retention & Deletion

Personal data must only be retained for the duration required to deliver contracted services or meet regulatory retention requirements.

DPA Agreement & Acceptance

Vendors must formally acknowledge and accept this DPA prior to onboarding or processing any government or regulated data.
